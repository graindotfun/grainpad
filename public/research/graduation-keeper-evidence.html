Graduation evidence.

Graduation to Raydium CPMM is handled by Raydium's automated migration, which Raydium support confirmed applies to all LaunchLab platforms. Our audit: 25/25 sampled third-party launches (SOL and wXMR) migrated within seconds. Confirmed by Raydium support, not a contractual guarantee.

Raydium support confirmation

Our automated migration process does not distinguish between platforms. As long as a token completes its sale during the Launch phase, it will automatically be migrated to CPMM. Migration usually completes within 0–1 slot.

Source: Raydium support Discord ticket, 29 September 2026, 07:55 Israel time. Statement supplied by Grainpad’s founder; the private ticket has not been independently accessed or publicly linked. The 0–1 slot estimate is support’s typical timing, not a deadline or contractual guarantee. This statement is separate from the on-chain audit below.

Read-only Solana mainnet audit · 29 September 2026

25 successful third-party migrations: 15 SOL and 10 wXMR, across six PlatformConfigs. All used RAYpQbFNq9i3mu6cKpTKKRwwHFDeK5AuZz8xvxUrCgw as the migration authority signer. Recorded delays were 0–1 seconds.

Scope of the evidence

The sample was selected from successful migrations in the authority’s transaction history. It does not measure the migration success rate of all completed curves or establish service for every platform. Raydium support separately confirmed automated migration applies to all LaunchLab platforms; this is not a contractual guarantee. There is no verified permissionless fallback.

Delays run from a successful TradeEvent marking the curve complete to successful MigrateToCpswap. Zero means the same recorded second, not zero physical latency. Every measured delay was below one hour.

A program-wide finalized scan at 2026-09-29T04:31:54.000Z, slot 451534753, found zero pools in the awaiting-migration state. This snapshot does not establish that no historical pool ever waited or that future pools cannot wait.
